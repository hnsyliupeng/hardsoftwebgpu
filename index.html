what you are looking at

A 710 mm three-segment soft continuum arm driven by nine tendons. Each joint is a chain of printed TRUNC metamaterial cells: a helical truss torque channel (52× stiffer in torsion than in bending) nested inside equatorial cells that guide the cables. The motor at the base spins the truss shaft through every bend, which is what lets the tool fasten bolts and turn valves while the structure stays soft enough to absorb misalignment.

The controller stack mirrors the paper: a learned inverse-kinematics network maps the desired tool pose to tendon lengths, a classical damped-least-squares solver fills in the residual, and a causal transformer policy sequences the whole task from the plan tokens and the object parameters. Train both here — datasets are distilled from the scripted expert in a Web Worker.
